# Scenarios

| Scenario ID | Scenario Title | Actor/Stakeholder | Scenario Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| S-01 | First-Time Setup | Student (new user) | A first-year student, Layla, hears about StudyBuddy from a classmate before finals week. She registers an account with her university email, creates her first deck titled "COE420 – Software Engineering," and manually adds three flashcards covering key definitions from her lecture notes. |
| S-02 | AI Flashcard Generation from Lecture Slides | Student | Two days before a midterm, a student uploads a 15-page PDF of lecture slides on database normalization. The system extracts the text and uses the OpenAI API to generate 12 candidate flashcards. The student reviews them, edits two that were phrased awkwardly, discards one that was irrelevant, and saves the rest to a new deck. |
| S-03 | Daily Spaced-Repetition Review | Student | A student opens StudyBuddy every morning during exam season. The system presents the cards that are due for review that day, in order determined by the spaced repetition algorithm. After each card, the student rates their recall as "Again," "Hard," "Good," or "Easy," and the system reschedules the card accordingly. |
| S-04 | Tracking Progress and Motivation | Student | A student who has been studying consistently for 12 days checks their dashboard before an exam. They see their current streak, the number of cards reviewed that week, and their accuracy percentage. They then check the leaderboard to see how their activity compares to classmates in the same course, which motivates them to complete one more review session that day. |
| S-05 | Reorganizing Study Material Across Devices | Student | A student who created several decks on their laptop logs into StudyBuddy from their phone between classes. They edit a deck's description, delete an outdated card, and merge two small decks into one before their next study session, with all changes reflected immediately regardless of device. |
